# NNNN. The decision, stated as a sentence

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Sources:** where the reasoning is recorded in this repository — a README
  section, a document, a code comment, a migration header. An ADR that cannot
  cite one is either new (say so) or inventing history.

## Context

What forced a decision: the constraint, the failure, or the question that could
not be left unanswered. Numbers belong here when they are what made it a
decision — a row count, a rate limit, a latency budget.

## Options considered

Each option with the strongest argument for it, and why it was not taken. An
option nobody would seriously propose is not worth listing; an option that was
rejected for a reason worth remembering is the point of this section.

## Decision

What was chosen and why, in a couple of sentences. If the choice was contingent
— on scale, on a dependency, on a deployment shape — say what it is contingent
on, because that is what a future reader needs in order to know when to revisit
it.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what has to change for the
decision to be revisited. Include the costs that were accepted deliberately;
they are the part people forget, and the part that turns into a surprise bug
report two years later.
